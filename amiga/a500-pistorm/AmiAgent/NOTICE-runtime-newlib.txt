Runtime notices for amiagent and amimon (newlib, bebbo's AmigaOS fork)
========================================================================

A built amiagent or amimon is statically linked with the C runtime that bebbo's
amiga-gcc ships prebuilt (lib/libc.a and lib/crt0.o, amiga-gcc commit 2f240d1,
17-07-2026), plus libnix's libstubs.a (public domain) and libgcc (GPL-3.0 with
the GCC Runtime Library Exception). Which objects are linked was read from
linker maps on 18-10-2026. The notices below are copied verbatim from the
sources of those objects in newlib-cygwin, branch amiga (commit 998257d; none
of these files changed after 17-07-2026).

STATUS. 20 AmigaOS-specific objects (crt0, malloc.cpp, open, __nocommandline,
__dosbase, __stdiowin, __mathieeedoubbas, dummy, the IEEE wrappers __adddf3,
__subdf3, __muldf3, __divdf3, __eqdf2, __fixdfsi, __floatsidf, __floatunsidf,
and __divsi3, __mulsi3, __udivsi3) carry no licence notice. Their author has
not yet confirmed the terms under which they may be redistributed. Until that
is settled, the binary disk image must not be distributed publicly. See
THIRD_PARTY.md in the source repository.

This product includes software developed by the University of California,
Berkeley and its contributors.
